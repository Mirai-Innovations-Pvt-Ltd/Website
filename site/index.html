Questions.

How much floor space does it need?

The station stands about 1.70 m tall. Leave 1.5–1.8 m clear in front: shoppers stand at a floor mark 1.5 m back, crews at 1.65 m, so the camera frames everyone head to toe.

How long does a try-on take?

About 20 seconds from pressing try-on to seeing yourself in the look.

How long does a pilot run?

As long as your drop or event needs. Our first ran for one day, at a brand pop-up in a mall; a run in store can go on for longer.

What do you need from us to start?

Your drop: product photos (the front, plus any other angles you have) and the name of each piece. We build the station's skin around your identity and handle install.

What happens to shoppers' photos?

Your capture is used only to render your looks. It is held in memory, never saved to disk, and wiped when you finish or walk away.

Does someone need to staff it?

Shoppers use it unaided. During a pilot, one of our team is on site to start it and keep it running.

Can friends try it together?

Yes. Crew mode captures you and your friends in one frame, so you can twin in matching fits.

Can it tie a try-on to a sale?

Yes. Each look can carry an optional referral code, so you can see which try-ons became purchases, at the till or online. Your readout also covers sessions, try-ons and the most-tried pieces.

What does it cost?

A campaign fee for the pilot. Tell us your venue and dates and we will send terms.
