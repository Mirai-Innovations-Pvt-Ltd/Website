Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy covers the Layer website and Layer try-on stations, both run by Mirai Innovations Private Limited (CIN U23101TN2025PTC184733), Chennai, India ("Mirai", "we"). We decide how this personal data is used, so we are its Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. We also follow the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

In short

  • At a Layer Station, your photo is used only for your try-on. It is held in the station's memory, never saved to disk, and wiped when your session ends.
  • We do not use face recognition. The camera is not used to guess your gender, age or size, and skin tone is never scored.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. We never sell personal data or use your photos to train AI models.

At a Layer Station

The camera. The camera is off on the start screen and turns on only after you touch to start; a green dot and the words CAMERA ACTIVE stay on screen whenever it is on. After a countdown it takes one full-length photo of you, or of you and your crew. It will not take the photo if someone is partly in frame or standing behind you.

What your photo is used for. Only your try-on:

  • The live view is checked on the station to frame the shot and never leaves it.
  • To order the garments for you, the station reads a few style attributes from the photo once, for the main person in it. They stay in memory, are wiped with the session and are never recorded. You choose the Women or Men catalogue yourself.
  • For each look, your photo and chosen garments go over the internet to a cloud image model run by Google, which returns the finished image. No name, session number or other identifier goes with it. Google may process it on servers outside India.

How long it is kept. Your photo and looks live only in the station's memory and are wiped when your session ends. If no one touches the screen for 60 seconds the station shows a warning; at 80 seconds it wipes everything and returns to the start screen, so walking away is enough. We keep no copy and cannot send you your photos later.

Prints. At events that offer them, a look is printed only when you ask. It is sent to the printer from memory and we keep no copy.

Anonymous session data. The station records what happened in a session, not who you are: pieces viewed and for how long, pieces tried on, the catalogue chosen, whether a print was made, and session counts and timings. Each session is a random ID with no images, names, phone numbers or style attributes. We use it to improve Layer and to give the hosting brand or venue a readout of what shoppers liked.

Friends and children. If friends are in your photo, make sure they are happy to be. Anyone under 18 should use the station only with a parent or guardian who agrees.

On this website

  • No tracking. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and serves its fonts itself. Our hosting provider may keep basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit) to keep the site secure and working.
  • The contact form. It asks for your name, role, company, city, phone, email and campaign dates. When you press send, your details go to our website host, Netlify, Inc., which stores the message and emails it to us; Netlify may process it outside India. If sending fails, the form instead writes a message that you can send yourself through WhatsApp or your own email app, and those providers handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • What we do with it. We use your details to reply and to plan and run a pilot with you. We keep them while we are in touch and as long as the law requires for business records, then delete them.

Who we share data with

  • Google, only to render your looks, as described above.
  • Netlify, our website host: hosting the site and delivering contact-form messages to us.
  • Brands and venues hosting a station: anonymous readouts only, never images or contact details.
  • Authorities, when Indian law requires it.

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Keeping it safe

Keys to the image service stay on our backend, never in the station's screen app or our logs. Shopper images never go into support tickets, and only our team can see contact messages. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us:

  • what personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with;
  • to correct, complete, update or erase it;
  • to stop using it by withdrawing your consent (at a station, end your session or walk away);
  • to let someone you nominate act for you if you die or cannot act yourself.

Because station photos are wiped and session data is anonymous, there is usually nothing from a station visit we can find or return. For anything you sent us from the website, write to us and we will reply within 30 days.
